02 / FAQ

Common questions

Everything you need to know about how Datapad's tools work.

01 Is Datapad free to use?

Yes. Every Datapad tool is completely free - no sign-up, no paywall, no premium tier, no usage limits. Every feature is available to every user, forever.

no-signup always-free no-limits
02 Does Datapad store or transmit my data?

No. All computation runs entirely in your browser via JavaScript. Your data never leaves your device - zero analytics or telemetry calls on tool inputs. Safe for sensitive payloads including production secrets, customer PII, and internal API responses.

client-side zero-tracking offline-capable
03 What tools does Datapad currently offer?

Three tools are live today: A/B Test Calculator, Pre/Post Impact Analyzer, and JSON Studio. The toolkit is actively growing - new developer and analyst utilities ship regularly. CSV Studio, Regex Playground, and a Sample Size Planner are next in queue.

3-live growing monthly-ships
04 A/B testing vs pre/post analysis?

A/B tests use concurrent randomized groups and can establish causation. Pre/post analysis compares sequential periods - it shows a change occurred, but cannot prove your intervention caused it. External factors like seasonality may be responsible. Use A/B testing when you can randomize traffic; use pre/post for site-wide changes.

causation correlation randomization
05 Why browser-based instead of cloud?

Three reasons: privacy (your data never leaves your device, safe for sensitive payloads), speed (no network round-trips), and reliability (works offline once loaded). It also means we don't need to charge subscription fees or run servers - so the tools stay free.

privacy speed offline
