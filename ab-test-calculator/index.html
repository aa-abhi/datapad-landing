How to Use This Calculator

1. Enter Your Data

Input the number of visitors (sample size) and conversions (successes) for your control group and at least one variant. For revenue tests, also add total revenue.

2. Configure Settings

Confidence Level: How sure you need to be. 95% is the industry standard — higher confidence = harder to reach significance.

Hypothesis: Two-sided tests whether the variant is better OR worse. One-sided only tests if it's better.

Variants: Support A/B, A/B/C, or A/B/C/D multivariate testing.

3. Read Your Results

P-Value: Probability this result occurred by chance. Below your alpha (0.05) = statistically significant.

Statistical Power: Probability of detecting a real effect if one exists. Aim for ≥80%.

MDE: Minimum Detectable Effect — the smallest change your test can reliably detect at 80% power.

Confidence Interval: The range where the true conversion rate most likely falls.

Relative Lift: Percentage change in conversion rate from control to variant.

Absolute Difference: Raw difference in conversion rates between variant and control.

4. Advanced Tabs

Power Analysis: See required sample sizes for various effect sizes, plus a visual power curve.

Monte Carlo Simulation: Runs 5,000 randomized trials to estimate the probability of winning, losing, or inconclusive outcomes — plus a full lift distribution.

Insights: Auto-generated plain-English recommendations based on your data, with projected business impact.

5. Export

Click "Export Excel" to download a multi-sheet .xlsx report with Summary, Insights, Simulation results, and Power Analysis tables.
